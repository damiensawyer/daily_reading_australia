I believe in God.

I believe that He loves me with a perfection and intensity beyond my ability to understand.

I believe that He is both three persons and one, and that the ability to understand this is also beyond me. It is held lovingly by Him, seemingly just out of my reach, so that I am ever drawn deeper into Him seeking more. When I choose to rest with what has been revealed to me, then I rest in satisfaction. When I choose to continue to explore His love and depths, there is always more to find. I believe that this will never end and receive it as a wonderful and eternal gift.

I believe that God sees all that I do, indeed all that is done. He doesn't watch me from a distance, but by His very will is keeping me in existence. He is closer to me than my breath, he is perfectly present to even my thoughts, indeed, closer to them than even I am, as He has perfect understanding of them which I do not. In every moment He is holding me close and willing that my next thought be aligned with His will.

I believe that this is what I was created for. I was given a mind, within a soul and the free will to choose to be a part of Him or apart from Him. This is the essence of my entire eternal existence. My life is a time to be trained and to choose. A time to learn how to align with Him.

Nothing is static, because God's creation is alive. All creation is in motion. I was created to move in perfect synchronization with Him. In my littleness and learning my path to Him moves sometimes closer and sometimes further away. This is for two reasons. In my humanity and ongoing formation, I am still learning to master moving in perfect union with my God. He rejoices in my smallness and my stumbles as I learn. In my imperfection though, I sometimes use my will to deliberately move away from Him.

I believe that others exist who have chosen to reject God and that they actively tempt me to do the same. With my free will, and entirely at my own fault, I often choose this path too.

I believe, from my many experiences, that when I choose to move away from God there is nothing to be found but misery, pain, anger and ultimately hate.

I believe that, whilst the choice for or against God's will is ultimately mine, I am weak, easily distracted and deceived.

I believe that in His love, God gives me an army of help. This help comes in the form of angels, as yet unseen be me but completely present, in the saints - my brothers and sisters who have gone before me, and from those people around me - the living church - who are also striving for the same goal as me of unity with God. I believe that - just as I am held in existence by God - so too are all of these helpers. All that is good, all that is love, ultimately comes from God. It comes through us, but is not from us. Any light that I or any of us might shine, is but a mirror reflecting the light from Him.

When I or another does not shine in a way visible to others, then we are not with God. For God is light, and in Him there is no darkness at all. To be aligned with God is to reflect His light. When our path to Him is obstructed, then we are in shadow and neither see nor reflect His true light.

I believe that creation is the unique domain of God, but that as His creation we can shape and change it. We can either focus or distort His light and that, in proportion to the alignment of our will to His, our remixing of His creation will be either good or evil.
